Day by day

Sun 23

Arrival day — straight into the clearest water on the coast

13:30

Check in at Via Fragalà, bags down. Pick up four cheap snorkel masks on Via Etnea on the way out — they earn their keep all week.

14:45

Walk to Catania Centrale and take bus 534 to Aci Trezza — 30 minutes, €1.40. Lava rock, the Cyclops stacks a hundred metres offshore, a marine protected area, and visibility that regularly runs past ten metres.Snorkel free off the rocks along Lungomare dei Ciclopi and beside Lido dei Ciclopi. No boat needed — the drop-offs are visible from the surface.

18:00

Aci Castello next door: a Norman castle built straight onto a black lava crag, and the cheapest good view of the week. Aperitivo at Caffè del Porto on the Trezza promenade.

21:00

Bus back (last one 22:40) or a taxi. Dinner: Antica Trattoria La Paglia by the fish market runs to midnight; Trattoria Achille on Via Plebiscito does horse-meat panini for €4.

22:30

Piazza Teatro Massimo and Piazza Bellini, six minutes from the door. Nothing in Sicily fills before half ten. Tonight is a warm-up — Monday is a long one.

Mon 24

Fish market, then the volcano at golden hour

08:30

La Pescheria, five minutes away. Swordfish heads, men shouting, blood on the cobbles — go before nine while it's still working rather than performing. Granita and brioche after at Comis Ice Cafè, Piazza Bellini, €3.80.

11:00

Out of the sun; pack for altitude. Long trousers, fleece and a windproof each, head torch for the lava cave. It will be 32°C in the street and 11–15°C on the mountain at dusk.

14:30

Etna sunset tour — pickup at the door, seven hours. Craters around 2,000 m, a lava tube, sunset off the flank, back about 21:30. The volcano is currently active — see the volcano notice under Info.

23:00

Afrobar, La Playa — "Tutti i Lunedì". The Monday beach party on the sand, guestlist entry, free grill with a drink until midnight. DM their Instagram in the afternoon for the list.Taxi €15–22 each way for four. Bus D stops at midnight and there is no night bus — budget the taxi both ways.

Tue 25

Car day one — Taormina, Isola Bella, Castelmola at sunset

09:00

Collect the car at the airport. Photograph every panel, wheel and the windscreen before leaving the bay — timestamps are the only defence in a damage dispute.

10:15

45 minutes up the A18 (€1.80 toll). Park at Lumbi above Taormina, free shuttle into town, then the funivia down to the water — €6 single, €10 return. The shuttle runs until 00:50, so no curfew.

10:45

Swim and snorkel at Isola Bella. Pebbles, no sand, the clearest water on this coast — the rocks either side of the spit are the good bit. The beach is free; the islet nature reserve is €6 to walk across.

15:00

Taormina. Teatro Antico, €14, last entry 19:00 — a Greek theatre with Etna framed in the broken stage wall. Then Corso Umberto for the passeggiata.

19:00

Castelmola for sunset — five kilometres of hairpins above town, or forty minutes on the Sentiero dei Saraceni on foot. Bar Turrisi is decorated floor to ceiling with phalluses and serves almond wine; the viewpoint at Piazza Sant'Antonio looks over Taormina, the coast and Etna. Dinner up there, then 45 minutes home.

Wed 26

Canyon pools at dawn, white sand by lunch

06:00

Out the door. Sunrise is 06:24; the canyon must be done in the cool hours. An hour and a quarter to the Belvedere di Avola Antica.

07:30

Cavagrande del Cassibile. €2 at the gate, card only, then a 300 m descent on rock steps — forty minutes down, an hour and a quarter back up. At the bottom: a chain of deep green freshwater pools in a limestone canyon, and at eight in the morning, quite possibly nobody else in them.Closed shoes are mandatory and staff check. Be climbing out by 10:30 — the canyon is airless by midday. Two litres of water each.

12:30

Twenty minutes to the coast for salt water and a cold beer. Fontane Bianche: white sand, beach bars, a rocky snorkelling corner. Quieter alternative: Gelsomineto, sand under pines, no facilities at all.

20:00

Recovery evening. Dinner at Nuova Trattoria del Forestiero, Via Coppola, €10–20 (book ahead), then Vermut on Via Gemmellaro — vermouth and natural wine, €5–7 a glass.

Thu 27

The Alcantara river day — rafting, plunge pools, Etna wine

08:15

Leave. An hour and ten to Motta Camastra via the A18 and SS185 — the last fifteen kilometres are twisty — allow the full time.

10:00

Body rafting, put-in at Agriturismo Valle Alcantara. Two hours, about ninety minutes in the water: scramble and swim upstream through the basalt, then ride the current back down, with around five jumps on the way. The river is spring-fed and 10–12°C all year; the 5 mm wetsuit provided is needed.

12:45

Ten minutes to Francavilla di Sicilia for lunch. Park at the Museo Archeologico or the rough spiazzo on Via Orsino Orsini.

14:00

Le Gurne dell'Alcantara. Sixteen lava plunge pools — some fifty metres across and ten deep — upstream of the tourist gorge. Free, no ticket, no guide, almost entirely locals. The signed path runs 1.5 km each way past the Mulino Ciappa; Gurna della Passarella is the one, a double waterfall into a big pool.Sun-exposed, so far warmer than the gorge. Wet basalt is slippery as soap: river shoes on, and no swimming directly under the falls.

15:30

The active route back: return down the riverbed itself — scrambling, wading and swimming the pools instead of the path. Turns a gentle stroll into a proper three-hour afternoon.

17:45

Fifteen minutes to Castiglione di Sicilia — officially one of Italy's most beautiful villages, castle on top, the whole Alcantara valley below and Etna behind. The Cuba di Santa Domenica on the way in is a tenth-century chapel: five minutes, free.

18:45

Etna DOC tasting ten minutes on at Passopisciaro — Cottanera from €40, or Graci, Pietradolce and Girolamo Russo nearby. Booked ahead; driver nominated at breakfast. Then an hour and a quarter home down the north flank.The famous narrow gorge is skippable, or €2 via the municipal Larderia staircase on the way past — 200 steps to the pebble beach for a quick wade. The private park package is the same water with a lift.

Fri 28

Ortigia by day, Villa Bellini by night

09:00

An hour south to Syracuse. Park at Talete on the edge of Ortigia — €1.50/hour, €15/day. Do not drive onto the island; the ZTL is effectively all-day.

10:30

Ortigia — the prettiest city of the week. A baroque cathedral built inside a Greek temple, the Fonte Aretusa, lanes narrow enough to stay cool. Then a sea-caves boat tour round the island: an hour for €25–30, two with prosecco for €40–50.

13:30

Swim off the rocks at Cala Rossa or Forte Vigliena — ladders straight into deep clear water. Lunch in the market lanes off Via Trento.

17:00

Leave by five, home by six.

21:00

ANNA live at Villa Bellini, twelve minutes' walk up Via Etnea. Then Afrobar's "We Love 2000s" at 23:00, €10 plus fee, on the sand. This is the biggest night of the week.

Sat 29

Best beach, best village, best granita — keys back by seven

07:00

An hour forty-five to Vendicari; park at the Calamosche gate, €3.50 entry, twenty-minute walk in.

09:00

Calamosche. Two hundred metres of sand between two rock headlands — no bar, no sunbeds, no road, and the best water in eastern Sicily. Full by half nine in August, hence the alarm. Bring all water and food in.

13:00

Marzamemi, half an hour on: an old tuna-fishery village where the whole square is tables and the whole menu is fish. Then the shallow turquoise sand at San Lorenzo, ten minutes away.

15:45

Noto on the way home, when the baroque goes the colour of honey. Granita at Caffè Sicilia on Corso Vittorio Emanuele — widely rated the best in Sicily.Hard rule: wheels rolling out of Noto by 17:30. It's an hour to the airport and the car is due at 19:00.

18:45

Drop the car — the airport is on the way in. Then the flat €27 taxi or the €4 Alibus into the centre.

23:00

Closing night, three options: Le Capannine — "Saturday Night @Switch", three rooms on the beach, €5–7 before midnight; Banacher in Aci Castello, open-air under palms, reservation only, taxi €20–28; or Afrobar's 80s/90s night.

Sun 30

Granita and departure

09:30

Last almond granita, a final arancino from Savia on Via Etnea (€2.50–4), half an hour in the shade of Villa Bellini. The fish market is shut Sundays.

10:30

Alibus to the airport, €4, twenty minutes — or the flat €27 taxi. Leave margin: Sunday runs a reduced timetable.

Food & drink directory

nothing over €20

Sit-down, walkable from the flat

  • Da Aldo, Via Sangiuliano 18 — €8–15, lunch only, cash, four minutes away. Arrive at opening or don't bother.
  • U Fucularu, near Piazza Università — pasta alla Norma €14, Catania's signature dish on home turf.
  • La Paglia, Via Pardo 23 — fish-market seafood, open to midnight daily. The late-night save.
  • Nuova Trattoria del Forestiero, Via Coppola — €10–20, book two days ahead.
  • Trattoria Achille, Via Plebiscito 734 — the horse-meat grill strip locals actually use. Panino €4.

Street food & sugar

  • Savia, Via Etnea 300 — arancini €2.50–4, since 1897. Catania's arancini are cone-shaped.
  • Scirocco Fish Lab, in the fish market — fried-fish cones €6–7.
  • Panetteria Pacini — arancini under €5, the local's local.
  • Comis Ice Cafè, Piazza Bellini — granita + brioche €3.80. Tear the brioche and dip it — the local way to eat it.
  • Caffè Sicilia, Noto — the granita all the others are measured against. Saturday's reward.

On the road

  • Anchovy Fish Bar, Aci Trezza — seafood sandwiches ~€13 on the harbour.
  • Il Covo Marino, Aci Trezza — sit-down with dock views, ~€20.
  • Marzamemi — any table on the piazza; order whatever the boat brought in.
  • Bar Turrisi, Castelmola — almond wine — the bar is known for its eccentric décor.
  • Cottanera, Passopisciaro — Etna DOC tasting from €40, booked ahead.

The bar shortlist

  • A Putìa dell'Ostello, Piazza Currò — cocktails, weekend DJ sets to dawn. Best all-rounder.
  • Vermut, Via Gemmellaro 39 — vermouth and natural wine, €5–7.
  • First Lounge Bar, Piazza Goliarda Sapienza — regenerated San Berillo, the most interesting crowd in town.
  • Ciclope (craft beer) and Stratosferica (cocktails), both by Piazza Bellini.
  • Via del Plebiscito below Castello Ursino — street food into late drinking, peaks 01:00–03:00.

House rules for club nights

  • Timings are Sicilian: aperitivo 19:00–22:00, bars fill 22:30, clubs peak 01:00–04:00. Arriving from 23:00 is normal.
  • Afrobar, Capannine and Banacher run these as weekly residencies — confirm on Instagram the day before, and DM for guestlist.
  • Banacher requires a reservation and has a smarter dress code than the beach clubs.
  • Spritz €5–8, cocktails €5–12, beach-club entry €10–20 usually with a drink.

Essential information

practical
Volcano notice

Etna has been erupting since 6 August and is still active — which is why the Monday tour may be spectacular, and also why plans can change at short notice. Excursions above 2,500 m require a licensed guide; the tour handles all of that. Check flight status the evening before both flights, and follow the operator's guidance if the tour is rescheduled.

The car

  • Parking space: Via della Loggetta 11. Navigate to the parking, never the flat — the flat sits on the edge of a camera-enforced restricted zone that operates 24/7, all year.
  • Petrol €1.99/litre; always the fai da te pump. Budget €70–85 across the five days, split four ways.
  • A18 tolls: Catania–Taormina €1.80.
  • Leave nothing visible in the parked car.
  • Add 20–40 minutes to satnav estimates.

Pickpockets work the fish market, Via Etnea crowds and buses — phones off café tables. Late at night stay on the Etnea–Duomo–Teatro Massimo axis, which is busy and lit.

Packing addendum

  • River shoes or old trainers — mandatory at Cavagrande (they check), essential in the Gurne riverbed.
  • Snorkel masks — bought on Via Etnea, day one.
  • Fleece, windproof, long trousers, head torch — Monday's volcano kit.
  • Two-litre water bottle each for Wednesday; dry bag for Thursday.
  • Cash for the rafting balance; a card for Cavagrande, which takes nothing else.

Timekeeping

  • The fish market closes Sundays and Mondays by lunch; most museums close Mondays; shops shut 13:30–16:30.
  • Sunday buses run a reduced timetable — affects arrival and departure days.
  • Restaurants fill at 21:30. Booking two days out is the local norm for anywhere good.
  • Saturday's fixed point: car back at the airport by 19:00.
