The one driving rule

Your Loggetta space is outside the restricted zone, so the fines aren't a worry — as long as you never set the satnav to the flat. Always navigate to Via della Loggetta, then walk. ZTL Bellini is camera-enforced 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, with no evening or weekend window, and Via Fragalà sits right on the edge of it.

Tuesday morning: leave at 08:00, walk 12 minutes to Centrale, Alibus at 08:20 (€4, every 20 min), desk by 09:00. Saturday's 19:00 drop is the hard deadline the last day is built backwards from.

Before anything else

four calls
Book today — it's the best night of your week

ANNA at Villa Bellini, Friday 28th, 21:00, from €45. Anna Pepe, twelve minutes up Via Etnea from your door. The only headline act that week aimed at your age group — the rest of the Sotto il Vulcano bill is Fiorella Mannoia and Massimo Ranieri. Friday's day plan is built to get you back from Syracuse in time.

Etna is still erupting — your tour is the right booking anyway

The Voragine eruption began 6 August and shut the airport for five days; it reopened on the 15th. INGV's 18 August bulletin still reports Strombolian activity, ash to ~6,000 m and lava reaching down to ~1,330 m in the Valle del Bove, with tremor falling. Mayors have imposed a 200 m standoff from lava fronts, ten people per guide, and automatic closure of the summit zones if the alert rises.

Your 14:30 tour works around 2,000 m — the part still open, and with lava flowing, the best show on the mountain. Message the operator on Sunday to confirm, and check your outbound flight the night before you fly.

Three bits of sequencing logic. Cyclops snorkelling moves to Sunday because it's the one thing you want that needs a bus, not a car — which frees a whole car day for the Alcantara. Wednesday's canyon needs a 06:00 start, so it follows the week's one quiet night. And Friday and Saturday both drive home past the airport, since Fontanarossa sits south of the city — so Saturday's 19:00 drop happens en route, not as an errand.

Day by day

eight days
Sun 23

Land, then straight into the clearest water on the coast

13:30

Transfer in, bags down. Buy four cheap snorkel masks on Via Etnea on the way out — you'll use them all week.

14:45

Walk to Centrale and take bus 534 to Aci Trezza — 30 minutes, €1.40. Lava rock, the Cyclops stacks a hundred metres offshore, a marine protected area, and visibility that regularly runs past ten metres.Snorkel free off the rocks along Lungomare dei Ciclopi and beside Lido dei Ciclopi. No boat needed — you can see the drop-offs from the surface.

18:00

Aci Castello next door: a Norman castle built straight onto a black lava crag, and the cheapest good view of the week. Aperitivo after at Caffè del Porto on the Trezza promenade.

21:00

Bus back (last one 22:40) or a taxi, then dinner. Antica Trattoria La Paglia by the fish market runs to midnight; Trattoria Achille on Via Plebiscito does horse-meat panini for €4.

22:30

Piazza Teatro Massimo and Piazza Bellini, six minutes from the door. Nothing in Sicily fills before half ten. Keep tonight moderate — Monday is long.

Mon 24

Fish market, then the volcano at golden hour

08:30

La Pescheria, five minutes away. Swordfish heads, men shouting, blood on the cobbles — go before nine while it's still working rather than performing. Granita and brioche after at Comis Ice Cafè, Piazza Bellini, €3.80.

11:00

Out of the sun and pack for altitude: long trousers, fleece and a windproof each, plus a head torch for the lava cave. It'll be 32°C in the street and 11–15°C on the mountain at dusk.

14:30

Etna sunset tour — pickup at the door, seven hours. Craters around 2,000 m, a lava tube, sunset off the flank, back about 21:30.

23:00

Afrobar, La Playa — "Tutti i Lunedì". Their weekly beach party, guestlist entry, free grill with a drink until midnight. DM them on Instagram in the afternoon to get on the list.Taxi €15–22 each way for the four of you. Bus D stops at midnight and there is no night bus back.

Tue 25

Car keys, then Taormina and the best snorkelling in the north

09:00

Collect the car. Photograph every panel, wheel and the windscreen before you leave the bay — Catania's cheaper desks have form for damage charges and timestamps are the only defence. Expect a hold of €1,200–2,000 on the lead driver's credit card if you've bought cover elsewhere.

10:15

Mazzarò and Isola Bella, 45 minutes up the A18 (€1.80 toll). Park at Lumbi above Taormina and take its free shuttle in, then the funivia down to the water — €6 single, €10 return. The shuttle runs until 00:50, so there's no rush at the end.

10:45

Swim and snorkel at Isola Bella. Pebbles, no sand, and the clearest water on this coast — the rocks either side of the spit are the good bit. The beach is free; the islet nature reserve is €6 if you want to walk across.

15:00

Taormina. Teatro Antico is €14, last entry 19:00 — a Greek theatre with Etna framed in the broken stage wall, worth it once. Corso Umberto afterwards.The €7 concession is EU citizens 18–25 only, so post-Brexit assume full price.

19:00

Castelmola for sunset — five kilometres of hairpins above town, or forty minutes on the Sentiero dei Saraceni if you've got legs. Bar Turrisi is decorated floor to ceiling with phalluses and serves almond wine; the view from Piazza Sant'Antonio is the real reason to go. Dinner up there, then 45 minutes home.

Wed 26

Canyon pools at dawn, white sand by lunch

06:00

Out the door — sunrise is 06:24 and this is the one early start that genuinely pays. An hour and a quarter to the Belvedere di Avola Antica, up a hairpin road above Avola.

07:30

Cavagrande del Cassibile. €2 at the gate, card only, then a 300 m descent on steep rock steps — forty minutes down, an hour and a quarter back up. At the bottom, a chain of deep green freshwater pools in a limestone canyon, and at eight in the morning quite possibly no one else.Closed shoes are mandatory and staff enforce it. Be climbing out by 10:30 — the canyon floor is airless, the ascent in 34°C has hospitalised people, and a hiker died here in June. Two litres of water each.

12:30

Twenty minutes to the coast for salt water and a cold beer. Fontane Bianche for white sand, beach bars and a rocky snorkelling corner. Gelsomineto is the better beach and much quieter — sand with pine shade — but has no facilities at all.

20:00

Dinner at Nuova Trattoria del Forestiero on Via Coppola, €10–20 (book two days out), then a slow one at Vermut, Via Gemmellaro 39 — vermouth and natural wine, €5–7 a glass. Nothing is on in town tonight, which is what your legs want.

Thu 27

A full day in the Alcantara — the river, not the turnstile

08:15

Leave. An hour and ten to Motta Camastra via the A18 and the SS185 — the last fifteen kilometres are twisty, so don't cut it fine.

10:00

Body rafting, put-in at Agriturismo Valle Alcantara, 750 m past the gorge entrance. Two hours, about ninety minutes in the water: you scramble and swim upstream through the basalt, then ride it back down, with around five jumps on the way. Spring-fed, so 10–12°C all year — after four days of Sicilian August that's the entire point.Hydrospeed only runs November to May, so it's not an option this week.

12:45

Ten minutes to Francavilla di Sicilia for lunch. Park at the Museo Archeologico or the rough spiazzo on Via Orsino Orsini.

14:00

Le Gurne dell'Alcantara. Sixteen lava plunge pools, some fifty metres across and ten deep, gouged into the basalt upstream of the tourist gorge. Free, no ticket, no guide, and almost entirely locals. The signed path runs about 1.5 km each way with nine numbered viewpoints, crossing an Enel footbridge past the Mulino Ciappa. Gurna della Passarella is the one — double waterfall into a big pool; the Laghetto di Venere also swims well.Because they're sun-exposed these are far warmer than the gorge. Wet basalt is slippery as soap, so river shoes, and don't swim directly under the falls.

15:30

The active version, since you asked for it: walk the trail out, then come back down in the riverbed — scrambling, wading and swimming the pools rather than the path. That turns a soft 3 km stroll into a proper three-hour afternoon, and it's what the walk is actually for.

17:45

Fifteen minutes to Castiglione di Sicilia, one of the borghi più belli d'Italia at 621 m, with the Castello di Lauria and the whole Alcantara valley below and Etna behind. Park at the bottom and walk up. The Cuba di Santa Domenica on the way in is a tenth-century Islamic-Norman chapel, five minutes and free.

18:45

Etna DOC wine, ten minutes on at Passopisciaro or Solicchiata — Cottanera does tastings from €40, or Graci, Pietradolce and Girolamo Russo are all within a few kilometres. Book ahead, these aren't walk-in, and settle the driver first. Then an hour and a quarter home down the north flank.Skip the famous narrow gorge, or do it on the way past for €2 via the municipal Larderia staircase — 200-odd steps to the pebble beach and a free wade. The private park's lift is €10–13 and the €30 ticket is a guided package with boots you won't need. Both entrances reach the same water; the public beach is mobbed in August, and you'll have already done the river properly.

Fri 28

Ortigia by day, Villa Bellini by night

09:00

An hour south to Syracuse. Park at Talete on the edge of Ortigia — €1.50/hour, €15 a day. Don't drive onto the island: the ZTL runs 11:00–15:30 and 17:00–02:00, which is effectively all day.

10:30

Ortigia — the one genuinely beautiful city of the trip. A baroque cathedral built inside a Greek temple, the Fonte Aretusa and its papyrus, lanes narrow enough to stay cool. Then a sea-caves boat tour round the island: an hour for €25–30, two with prosecco for €40–50.

13:30

Swim off the rocks at Cala Rossa or by Forte Vigliena — ladders straight into deep clear water, which is how Ortigia does beaches. Lunch in the market lanes off Via Trento.

17:00

Leave by five, home by six, past the airport, no drama.

21:00

ANNA at Villa Bellini, twelve minutes up Via Etnea. Then straight to Afrobar's "We Love 2000s" at 23:00, €10 plus fee, on the sand at La Playa. This is the night — leave nothing in reserve, Saturday looks after itself.

Sat 29

Best beach, best village, best granita — keys back by seven

07:00

Painful, worth it. An hour forty-five to Vendicari; park at the Calamosche gate, €3.50 entry, twenty-minute walk in.

09:00

Calamosche. Two hundred metres of sand between two rock headlands, no bar, no sunbeds, no road — the best water in eastern Sicily and the reason to have a car at all. Full by half nine in August, hence the alarm. Carry all your own water and food.

13:00

Marzamemi, half an hour on: an old tuna-fishery village where the whole square is tables and the whole menu is fish. Then the shallow turquoise sand at San Lorenzo, ten minutes away.

15:45

Noto on the way home, when the baroque goes the colour of honey. Granita at Caffè Sicilia on Corso Vittorio Emanuele — a genuine pilgrimage, not a tourist trap.Hard rule: leave Noto by 17:30. It's an hour to the airport and the car is due at 19:00.

18:45

Drop the car — the airport is on your way in. Then the flat €27 taxi or the €4 Alibus into the centre.

23:00

Last night, three ways. Le Capannine — "Saturday Night @Switch", three rooms of r'n'b, reggaeton, trap and latin on the beach, €5–7 before midnight. Banacher in Aci Castello for the smarter open-air version, reservation only, taxi €20–28. Or Afrobar's 80s/90s night if Friday went well.

Sun 30

Granita and out

09:30

Last almond granita and a final arancino from Savia on Via Etnea, €2.50–4. Half an hour in the shade of Villa Bellini. The fish market is shut on Sundays.

10:30

Alibus, €4, twenty minutes — or the flat €27 taxi. Leave margin: Sunday runs a reduced timetable and the ash backlog may still be clearing.

Numbers worth knowing

practicals

Taxis

Card payment is mandatory now, and two flat fares save you a fortune:

€12
Flat, anywhere in the historic centre, 20:00–05:00, up to four people. Valid all year — this covers most of your nights.
€27
Flat, Piazza Duomo or the station to the airport.
€15–22
Centre to La Playa and Afrobar, metered, at night.
€20–28
Centre to Aci Castello and Banacher.

FreeNow and appTaxi both work. Uber only dispatches licensed taxis here; no Bolt. No night bus back from any club.

Driving

  • Navigate to Via della Loggetta, never the flat. ZTL Bellini is enforced 24/7, all year.
  • Petrol is €1.99/litre. Always the fai da te pump — servito adds €0.14–0.20, motorway pumps reach €2.60. Budget €70–85 of fuel across the five days.
  • A18 tolls are trivial: Catania–Taormina is €1.80. Add 20–40 minutes to whatever Google says.
  • Nothing visible left in the car, anywhere, ever.

Kit worth having

  • River shoes or old trainers — mandatory at Cavagrande, essential in the Gurne riverbed.
  • Snorkel masks, bought cheap on Via Etnea on day one.
  • Fleece, windproof and long trousers for Monday's tour; head torch for the lava cave.
  • Two-litre water bottles each for Wednesday, and a dry bag for the river.
  • Cash for the body rafting balance and the Larderia gate; a card for Cavagrande, which takes nothing else.
