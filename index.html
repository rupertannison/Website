Day by day

Sun 23

Arrival day — first swim at Aci Trezza

In short: land, drop bags, bus up the coast for snorkelling among the Cyclops rock stacks, aperitivo by the castle, first night out near the flat.
flat → bus 534, 30 min, €1.40 → Aci Trezza → 10 min walk → Aci Castello → bus/taxi 25 min → home
13:30

Check in at Via Fragalà. Pick up cheap snorkel masks on Via Etnea on the way out — used all week.

15:00

Bus 534 from Catania Centrale to Aci Trezza. Lava-rock coast, the three Cyclops stacks just offshore, marine protected area, visibility often 10 m+. Snorkel free from the rocks on Lungomare dei Ciclopi.

18:00

Aci Castello: Norman castle on a black lava crag, best free view of the day. Aperitivo at Caffè del Porto back on the Trezza seafront.

21:30

Dinner near the flat — Antica Trattoria La Paglia (open to midnight) or horse-meat panini at Trattoria Achille, €4.Last 534 bus back is 22:40; after that it’s a taxi (~€20).

23:00

Piazza Teatro Massimo / Piazza Bellini, six minutes from the door. Bars fill from 22:30 and run late.

Mon 24

Fish market, then the volcano at sunset

In short: slow morning, the famous fish market before lunch, then the booked Etna tour from 14:30 — craters, a lava cave and sunset at 2,000 m — straight into the Monday beach party.
flat → tour pickup 14:30 at the door → Etna south flank (~1h) → drop-off ~21:30 → taxi 15 min, €15–22 → Afrobar, La Playa
10:30

Granita and brioche at Comis Ice Cafè (€3.80), then La Pescheria fish market, five minutes away — it runs until about 13:00.

13:00

Light lunch and pack for altitude: long trousers, fleece, windproof, head torch. 32°C in town, 11–15°C up there at dusk.

14:30

Etna sunset tour booked — seven hours: craters around 2,000 m, a lava tube, local produce and wine, sunset on the flank. The volcano is currently active — see the notice under Info.

23:00

Afrobar, La Playa — “Tutti i Lunedì” beach party pay there — guestlist via Instagram DM on the day; free grill with a drink before midnight. Runs well past 02:00. Taxi both ways.

Tue 25

Collect the car — Isola Bella, Taormina, Castelmola

In short: pick up the car, drive north for a swim and snorkel at Isola Bella, the Greek theatre in Taormina in the afternoon, sunset and dinner in the hill village above.
flat → Alibus 20 min, €4 → airport (car) → A18 45 min, toll €1.80 → Mazzarò/Isola Bella → funivia 5 min → Taormina → 15 min drive → Castelmola → 50 min → home
10:00

Alibus to the airport, collect the car (booked from the morning — tell the desk you’re coming late so the car is held; photograph every panel and wheel before leaving the bay).

12:00

Isola Bella: pebble beach, the clearest water on this coast, best snorkelling either side of the sand spit. Beach free; the islet reserve is €6 pay there. Lunch at a Mazzarò beach bar.

16:00

Funivia up to Taormina (€10 return pay there). Teatro Antico, €14 pay there, last entry 19:00 — the Greek theatre with Etna framed in the stage wall. Corso Umberto for the evening stroll.

19:15

Drive up to Castelmola for sunset over the whole coast. Almond wine at Bar Turrisi (known for its eccentric décor), dinner in the village, then 50 minutes home. Nightcap in town if there’s life left.

Wed 26

Beach first, canyon pools in the cool of the afternoon

In short: white-sand beach and lunch at Fontane Bianche, then the Cavagrande canyon in the late afternoon when the heat drops — a steep walk down to a chain of natural freshwater pools, swim, climb out before dusk.
flat → 55 min → Fontane Bianche → 25 min → Cavagrande belvedere (Avola Antica) → 1h15 → home
10:30

Drive south. Fontane Bianche: white sand, shallow turquoise water, beach bars for lunch. Quieter option 10 minutes on: Gelsomineto, sand under pines, no facilities.

15:00

Up to the Belvedere di Avola Antica. Entry €2, card only pay there, last entry 19:00. Ring 0931 67450 the day before — the reserve closes on red fire-risk days.

15:30

Descend Cavagrande del Cassibile: 300 m down on rock steps, about 40 minutes. Closed shoes are mandatory and checked. At the bottom, deep green pools between limestone walls — swim as long as the legs allow.

18:00

Start the climb out (about 1h15) — done by 19:15, before the light goes. Two litres of water each.

21:30

Dinner at Nuova Trattoria del Forestiero book ahead (book two days ahead), then Vermut on Via Gemmellaro. Gentler night — Thursday is full.

Thu 27

The Alcantara river day — rafting, plunge pools, a hill village

In short: wetsuits on at midday to swim and scramble up a basalt gorge, lunch in Francavilla, then a free afternoon in the Gurne — sixteen lava plunge pools almost nobody visits — and sunset from Castiglione.
flat → 1h10 (A18 + SS185, twisty) → Motta Camastra → 10 min → Francavilla → on foot to the Gurne → 15 min → Castiglione → 1h15 → home
10:15

Leave — allow the full drive time, the last stretch is hairpins.

12:00

Body rafting book ahead, €45 pp, ~2 hours: wetsuit, helmet, buoyancy aid and river shoes provided. Swim upstream through the basalt canyon, then ride the current back down with jumps on the way. River is 10–12°C all year.Book the 12:00 slot by phone/WhatsApp (Sicily Adventure or Body Rafting Alcantara). Ask for body rafting, not river trekking. Cash for the balance.

14:15

Lunch in Francavilla di Sicilia; park at the Museo Archeologico.

15:15

Le Gurne: sixteen lava plunge pools upstream of the tourist gorge — free, no ticket, no guide, mostly locals. Signed path 1.5 km each way; Gurna della Passarella (double waterfall) is the best swim. Optional active route back: down the riverbed itself, wading and swimming pool to pool.Wet basalt is very slippery — river shoes on, no swimming directly under the falls.

18:15

Castiglione di Sicilia — one of Italy’s listed most-beautiful villages, castle on top, the whole valley and Etna in view. Aperitivo or early dinner, then home down the coast.

Fri 28

Syracuse & Ortigia, back for the biggest night

In short: the most beautiful old city in eastern Sicily — baroque lanes, a boat trip into the sea caves, swimming off the rocks — home by 18:30 for the ANNA concert and the 2000s beach party.
flat → 1h (A18 south) → Talete car park, Ortigia → 1h back → home → 12 min walk → Villa Bellini → taxi 15 min → Afrobar
10:00

Drive south. Park at Talete (€1.50/h, €15/day) on the edge of Ortigia — the island itself is a restricted zone nearly all day.

11:15

Ortigia: the cathedral built inside a Greek temple, Fonte Aretusa, the market lanes off Via Trento for lunch.

14:00

Sea-caves boat tour round the island pay there — ~€25–30 for an hour, booked on the quay. Then swim off the rocks at Cala Rossa or Forte Vigliena — ladders into deep, clear water.

17:30

Drive home — in by 18:30, shower, eat something substantial.

21:00

ANNA live at Villa Bellini book ahead, from €45 (TicketOne/TicketSMS) — twelve minutes’ walk up Via Etnea.

23:30

Afrobar “We Love 2000s” pay there, €10 + fee, on the sand at La Playa. Runs past 02:00; taxi both ways.

Sat 29

South-east grand tour — car back by 19:00

In short: the best beach in eastern Sicily, a long fish lunch in a tuna-fishery village, golden-hour granita in baroque Noto — dropping the car at the airport on the way home. The 19:00 return is the one hard deadline of the week.
flat → 1h45 → Calamosche gate → 30 min → Marzamemi → 30 min → Noto → 1h → airport (drop car by 19:00) → taxi €27 / Alibus €4 → home
10:00

Leave on time today. Calamosche (Vendicari reserve): €3.50 pay there, 20-minute walk in, sand between two rocky headlands, no facilities — bring water and snacks.Arriving midday the small car park may be full; fallback is San Lorenzo beach 15 minutes south, with lidos and easy parking.

14:00

Marzamemi: former tuna fishery, the whole piazza is restaurant tables. Long seafood lunch.

16:15

Noto at golden hour, when the baroque stone glows. Granita at Caffè Sicilia — widely rated the best in Sicily.

17:30

Leave Noto. Fuel the car (full-to-full) just before the airport; drop by 19:00. Flat-rate taxi €27 or Alibus €4 into town.

23:00

Closing night: Le Capannine “Saturday Night @Switch” pay there (€5–7 before midnight, three rooms on the beach) or Banacher book ahead in Aci Castello (reservation required, smarter dress, taxi €20–28). Both run to 02:00+.

Sun 30

Departure

In short: the one morning the 10:00 rule can’t hold — for a midday flight, be in the taxi by about 09:45.
flat → taxi 25 min, flat €27 (or Alibus €4, 12 min walk + 20 min ride) → airport, ~2h before the flight
09:00

Pack, and a last arancino from Savia if it’s wanted (€2.50–4, opens early). The fish market is closed Sundays.

09:45

Taxi to the airport. Sunday runs reduced bus timetables, so the flat-fare taxi is the safe choice.

Food & drink directory

nothing over €20

Sit-down, walkable from the flat

  • Da Aldo, Via Sangiuliano 18 — €8–15, lunch only, cash, four minutes away. Arrive at opening.
  • U Fucularu, near Piazza Università — pasta alla Norma €14, Catania’s signature dish.
  • La Paglia, Via Pardo 23 — fish-market seafood, open to midnight daily.
  • Nuova Trattoria del Forestiero, Via Coppola — €10–20, book two days ahead.
  • Trattoria Achille, Via Plebiscito 734 — the horse-meat grill strip. Panino €4.

Street food & sweet things

  • Savia, Via Etnea 300 — arancini €2.50–4, since 1897. Catania’s arancini are cone-shaped.
  • Scirocco Fish Lab, in the fish market — fried-fish cones €6–7.
  • Panetteria Pacini — arancini under €5.
  • Comis Ice Cafè, Piazza Bellini — granita + brioche €3.80. Tear the brioche and dip it.
  • Caffè Sicilia, Noto — the benchmark granita, Saturday’s stop.

On the road

  • Anchovy Fish Bar, Aci Trezza — seafood sandwiches ~€13 on the harbour.
  • Il Covo Marino, Aci Trezza — sit-down with dock views, ~€20.
  • Marzamemi piazza — any table; order what the boats landed.
  • Bar Turrisi, Castelmola — almond wine and the view.
  • Mazzarò beach bars — lunch by the water on Taormina day.

Club night practicals

  • Timings are Sicilian: bars fill 22:30, clubs peak 01:00–04:00. Arriving from 23:00 is normal.
  • Afrobar, Capannine and Banacher run weekly residencies — confirm the night on Instagram the day before and DM for guestlist.
  • Banacher needs a reservation and a smarter dress code than the beach clubs.
  • Spritz €5–8 · cocktails €5–12 · beach-club entry €10–20, usually with a drink.
  • €12 flat taxi anywhere in the historic centre 20:00–05:00, up to four people — the going-out rate.

Essential information

practical
Volcano notice

Etna has been erupting since 6 August and remains active. The Monday tour operates at around 2,000 m under licensed guides and adapts its route daily. Check flight status the evening before both flights, confirm the tour with the operator on Sunday, and follow their guidance if anything is rescheduled.

The car

  • Parking space: Via della Loggetta 11. Navigate to the parking, never the flat — the flat borders a camera-enforced restricted zone active 24/7.
  • Petrol ~€1.99/litre; use the fai da te (self-service) pump. Budget €70–85 for the week, split four ways.
  • A18 tolls: Catania–Taormina €1.80. Keep coins/card handy.
  • Leave nothing visible in the parked car.
  • Add 20–40 minutes to satnav estimates.

Packing

  • River shoes or old trainers — required at Cavagrande (checked at the gate), essential in the Gurne.
  • Snorkel masks — buy cheap on Via Etnea, day one.
  • Fleece, windproof, long trousers, head torch — Monday’s volcano kit.
  • 2 L water each for Wednesday; dry bag for Thursday.
  • Cash for the rafting balance; a bank card for Cavagrande (card only).
